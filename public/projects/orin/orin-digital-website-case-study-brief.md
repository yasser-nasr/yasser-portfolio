# Orin — Portfolio Case Study Brief

## Project Type
Website Design + LinkedIn Cover + Custom Website Section Visuals

## Project Name
Orin

## Project Summary
Orin is a UAE-based consultancy firm providing integrated business setup, financial advisory, bookkeeping, VAT support, and compliance services.

For the portfolio, present this project only as a digital design project focused on:
- Website UI/UX design
- Responsive website experience
- LinkedIn cover design
- Custom visuals created for key website sections

Do not present this as a full branding case study.

## Portfolio Positioning
The work should communicate that the website was designed to make Orin feel:
- Clear
- Structured
- Premium-accessible
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Professional but approachable

The digital experience should reflect Orin's positioning as a long-term business and financial partner rather than a traditional setup or accounting office.

## Main Design Challenge
Orin offers multiple connected services across business setup, financial consultancy, bookkeeping, VAT, and compliance.

The design challenge was to organize those services into a clear digital experience that:
- Reduces complexity
- Makes the service offering easy to understand
- Builds trust
- Creates a premium but accessible visual impression
- Guides users toward consultation/contact actions

## Visual Direction
Use the existing Orin digital design language:
- Primary dark green: #1C3E2C
- Warm cream: #E8DFC0
- Muted sage: #6F8F7A
- Soft olive tint: #AEBFAF
- Charcoal: #2B2B2B
- Off-white: #F7F5EE
- Accent teal: #4FAF8F

Typography:
- Arial / clean sans-serif system

Visual tone:
- Spacious
- Editorial
- Calm
- Structured
- Minimal
- Premium-accessible

Avoid:
- Generic corporate stock photography
- Handshake visuals
- Bar-chart clichés
- Crowded layouts
- Overly sales-driven presentation
