## Project overview

Pillars PR is an Abu Dhabi-based marketing and public relations company specializing in communication for the legal sector across the MENA region.

I developed a connected visual identity and communication system that positioned the company as a specialist partner for lawyers, law firms, legal institutions, and legal education organizations. The system extended across brand identity, corporate documents, commercial communication, social media, logo motion, and video.

The objective was to bring legal credibility and contemporary communication together within one recognizable visual system.

## The challenge

Legal communication often depends heavily on traditional visual conventions, while general marketing agencies can lack the specialist character required to communicate with legal professionals.

Pillars PR needed an identity that could establish credibility within the legal sector without feeling outdated or institutional, while also communicating the modern marketing and public-relations capabilities behind the company.

The wider challenge was consistency. The identity needed to work across corporate presentations, service communication, commercial documents, social media, and motion while remaining recognizable as one brand.

## The approach

I built the identity around a structured visual language that connected the authority of the legal sector with the clarity of modern professional communication.

The system used a distinctive pillar-inspired brand motif, a controlled blue-led visual palette, clear information hierarchy, and a consistent balance between legal references and contemporary business communication.

Rather than treating each deliverable independently, I extended the same identity principles across corporate documents, social content, commercial communication, and motion.

## Brand positioning

Pillars PR was positioned as a specialist bridge between legal expertise and modern communication.

Instead of presenting the company as a general marketing agency, the identity supported a more focused proposition: marketing and public relations developed specifically for professionals and organizations within the legal sector.

**Case-study positioning summary:** Legal expertise, communicated with clarity.

This is not the official company tagline. The official tagline is “Building Legal Excellence.”

## Identity and visual language

The visual identity established the recognizable foundation for the wider communication system. A pillar-inspired motif connected the brand to ideas of structure and legal authority, while the blue-led direction and clear hierarchy kept communication modern and direct.

The logo and supporting graphic language were designed to remain consistent across different scales and formats, from corporate documents and commercial material to social content and motion.

## Corporate and commercial communication

The identity extended into a corporate company profile and commercial documents, including pricing material. These applications needed to organize detailed company, service, and commercial information with a credible and consistent hierarchy.

Using the same visual principles across these formats connected business communication back to the core identity instead of treating each document as a separate design.

## Social media system

The social-media design system translated the identity into repeatable content formats while preserving the same structured, specialist character.

This gave Pillars PR a recognizable visual presence for communicating with legal professionals and organizations across the UAE and wider region.

## Motion and video

The communication system also extended into logo animation, motion graphics, and video content. Motion gave the pillar-inspired visual language another format while maintaining continuity with the static identity.

Video design and editing supported the company’s broader communication needs without changing the design-led scope of my role.

## Outcome

The result was a connected identity and communication system that gave Pillars PR a consistent presence across corporate documents, commercial material, social media, and motion.

The visual system helped express the company's specialist legal-market positioning while creating enough flexibility to support different communication formats and audiences across the region.
